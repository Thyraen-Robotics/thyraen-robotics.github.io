What We Build

Autonomy that serves the mission

We develop autonomous systems that transform user objectives into coordinated operational effects across heterogeneous platforms and sensors at scale. By synchronizing machines as a cohesive team, our autonomy enables the warfighter to compress decision-to-effect timelines, sustain tempo, and execute operations in degraded and contested environments while remaining heads-up and in the fight — without added cognitive burden.
